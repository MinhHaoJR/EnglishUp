## Speaking and the senses
### say
declare|verb|B2|tuyên bố|to say something officially or firmly|The judge will declare the result tomorrow.
assert|verb|C1|khẳng định|to state something firmly|She continued to assert that she was innocent.
proclaim|verb|C2|công bố, tuyên bố|to announce something publicly|The king will proclaim a national holiday.
### ask
enquire|verb|C1|hỏi thăm, hỏi thông tin|to ask for information|I am writing to enquire about the course fees.
interrogate|verb|C1|thẩm vấn|to ask someone many questions, often officially|Police will interrogate the suspect tonight.
probe|verb|C2|thăm dò, điều tra kỹ|to try to find out the truth by asking questions|Reporters began to probe the minister's past.
solicit|verb|C2|xin, gạ gẫm|to ask someone for money, help or information|Charities solicit donations at Christmas.
### look
observe|verb|B2|quan sát|to watch carefully|Students observe the plants every day.
gaze|verb|C1|nhìn chăm chú|to look at something for a long time|We like to gaze at the stars at night.
stare|verb|B1|nhìn chằm chằm|to look at something without moving your eyes|It is rude to stare at strangers.
peer|verb|C1|nhìn kỹ, ngó|to look carefully because something is hard to see|He had to peer through the foggy window.
behold|verb|C2|ngắm nhìn (văn chương)|to see or look at (literary)|The sunrise was a sight to behold.
### see
perceive|verb|C1|nhận thấy|to notice or become aware of something|Dogs can perceive sounds we cannot hear.
detect|verb|B2|phát hiện|to discover something that is hard to see|The machine can detect tiny cracks.
discern|verb|C2|nhận ra, phân biệt|to notice something with difficulty|It was hard to discern the path in the dark.
witness|verb|B2|chứng kiến|to see something happen|Did anyone witness the accident?
### hear
overhear|verb|C1|nghe lỏm, tình cờ nghe|to hear what others say without them knowing|I didn't mean to overhear your call.
### think
contemplate|verb|C1|suy ngẫm, cân nhắc|to think about something carefully for a long time|She began to contemplate a move abroad.
ponder|verb|C1|trầm ngâm suy nghĩ|to think carefully before deciding|Take time to ponder the question.
ruminate|verb|C2|nghiền ngẫm|to think deeply about something for a long time|He would ruminate on his mistakes for days.
### feel
sense|verb|B2|cảm nhận|to feel something without being told|Children sense when their parents are worried.
endure|verb|C1|chịu đựng|to suffer something difficult for a long time|The villagers had to endure a long drought.
undergo|verb|C1|trải qua|to experience something, often unpleasant|He will undergo surgery next week.
## Everyday actions
### get
obtain|verb|B2|đạt được, thu được|to get something, often with effort|You must obtain a visa before you travel.
acquire|verb|B2|giành được, tiếp thu|to get or gain something|Children acquire language very quickly.
procure|verb|C2|thu mua, kiếm được|to get something, often with difficulty|The hospital struggled to procure enough masks.
derive|verb|C1|bắt nguồn, có được từ|to get something from a source|Many English words derive from Latin.
### give
bestow|verb|C2|ban tặng|to give an honour or gift|The university will bestow an award on her.
grant|verb|C1|cấp, ban cho|to officially give or allow something|The bank agreed to grant him a loan.
impart|verb|C2|truyền đạt|to pass on knowledge or a quality|Good teachers impart a love of learning.
### take
seize|verb|C1|chộp lấy, tịch thu|to take something quickly and firmly|Police seize fake goods at the border.
grasp|verb|C1|nắm chặt, nắm bắt|to hold firmly; to understand|Try to grasp the main idea first.
snatch|verb|C1|giật lấy|to take something quickly and roughly|A thief tried to snatch her bag.
extract|verb|C1|rút ra, chiết xuất|to remove or take something out|The dentist had to extract two teeth.
appropriate|verb|C2|chiếm đoạt|to take something for yourself without permission|He was accused of trying to appropriate public funds.
### go
proceed|verb|C1|tiến hành, đi tiếp|to continue or move forward|Passengers should proceed to gate five.
advance|verb|B2|tiến lên|to move forward|The army began to advance at dawn.
traverse|verb|C2|băng qua|to travel across an area|It took a week to traverse the desert.
### come
approach|verb|B1|tiến lại gần|to move closer|Don't approach the dog while it eats.
emerge|verb|C1|xuất hiện, lộ ra|to come out from somewhere|The sun began to emerge from the clouds.
converge|verb|C2|hội tụ|to come together at one point|Thousands of fans converge on the stadium.
### start
commence|verb|C1|bắt đầu (trang trọng)|to begin (formal)|The ceremony will commence at ten.
initiate|verb|C1|khởi xướng|to make something begin|The school will initiate a reading programme.
launch|verb|B2|ra mắt, khởi động|to start something new|The company will launch a new phone in May.
inaugurate|verb|C2|khánh thành|to open or begin something with a ceremony|The president will inaugurate the bridge.
### stop
cease|verb|C1|chấm dứt|to stop happening|The factory will cease production next year.
halt|verb|C1|dừng lại|to stop moving or happening|The train had to halt at the border.
terminate|verb|C1|chấm dứt, kết thúc|to end something officially|The company may terminate the contract.
discontinue|verb|C2|ngừng (sản xuất, cung cấp)|to stop making or providing something|They will discontinue this model next year.
suspend|verb|C1|tạm ngừng, đình chỉ|to stop something for a period|The airline had to suspend flights during the storm.
### use
utilize|verb|C1|tận dụng|to use something effectively|We must utilize every classroom.
employ|verb|B2|sử dụng; thuê|to use a method or skill; to give work to|Writers employ many tricks to keep readers interested.
exploit|verb|C1|khai thác; lợi dụng|to use fully, sometimes unfairly|Some companies exploit cheap labour.
### help
assist|verb|B2|hỗ trợ|to help someone|Volunteers assist the nurses at night.
aid|verb|C1|giúp đỡ, trợ giúp|to help or make something easier|Good sleep can aid memory.
facilitate|verb|C1|tạo điều kiện|to make something easier|Maps facilitate travel in a new city.
### show
demonstrate|verb|B2|chứng minh, trình bày|to show clearly how something works|The teacher will demonstrate the experiment.
illustrate|verb|B2|minh họa|to explain with examples or pictures|These figures illustrate the problem.
exhibit|verb|C1|trưng bày; biểu lộ|to show publicly or show a quality|The museum will exhibit her paintings.
manifest|verb|C2|biểu hiện|to show something clearly|Stress can manifest itself as headaches.
### find
unearth|verb|C2|khai quật, phát hiện|to dig up or discover something hidden|Archaeologists hope to unearth more coins.
ascertain|verb|C2|xác minh|to find out for certain|Police are trying to ascertain the cause of the fire.
locate|verb|B2|định vị, tìm ra vị trí|to find the exact position of something|The app helps you locate your phone.
### keep
retain|verb|C1|giữ lại|to continue to have something|The town has managed to retain its old charm.
maintain|verb|B2|duy trì|to keep something in good condition or at the same level|It is hard to maintain a steady speed uphill.
### change
alter|verb|B2|thay đổi, sửa|to change something slightly|The tailor can alter the dress.
modify|verb|B2|điều chỉnh|to change something to improve it|Engineers had to modify the design.
transform|verb|B2|biến đổi hoàn toàn|to change completely|Paint can transform an old room.
convert|verb|B2|chuyển đổi|to change from one form or use to another|They plan to convert the factory into flats.
evolve|verb|C1|tiến hóa, phát triển dần|to develop gradually|Languages evolve over centuries.
## Likes and needs
### love
cherish|verb|C1|trân trọng, nâng niu|to love and care for something deeply|I cherish the letters my grandmother wrote.
revere|verb|C2|tôn kính|to respect and admire deeply|Many people revere their teachers.
idolize|verb|C1|thần tượng|to admire someone too much|Teenagers often idolize pop stars.
venerate|verb|C2|sùng kính|to honour with great respect|Villagers venerate their ancestors.
### hate
detest|verb|C1|ghét cay ghét đắng|to hate very much|I detest waiting in long queues.
abhor|verb|C2|ghê tởm, căm ghét|to hate something for moral reasons|Most people abhor violence.
loathe|verb|C1|ghét cay đắng|to hate very much|Cats loathe getting wet.
despise|verb|C1|khinh miệt|to dislike and have no respect for|They despise people who cheat.
execrate|verb|C2|nguyền rủa|to feel or express great hatred for (formal)|Critics execrate the decision as cruel.
### like
appreciate|verb|B1|trân trọng, đánh giá cao|to value something|I really appreciate your help.
admire|verb|B1|ngưỡng mộ|to respect and approve of someone|Students admire teachers who are fair.
relish|verb|C1|thích thú, tận hưởng|to enjoy something very much|Some people relish a challenge.
savour|verb|C1|thưởng thức|to enjoy something slowly|Take a moment to savour the coffee.
### want
desire|verb|B2|khao khát|to want something strongly|Few people desire a life without friends.
crave|verb|C1|thèm muốn|to want something very strongly|Pregnant women often crave certain foods.
yearn|verb|C2|mong mỏi|to want something very much, often sadly|Migrants yearn for home at Tet.
aspire|verb|C1|khao khát vươn tới|to hope to achieve something great|Many students aspire to become doctors.
covet|verb|C2|thèm thuồng (thứ của người khác)|to want something that belongs to someone else|It is wrong to covet your neighbour's car.
### need
necessitate|verb|C2|đòi hỏi, cần phải có|to make something necessary|The new rules necessitate extra staff.
entail|verb|C1|kéo theo|to involve something as a necessary part|The job will entail some travel.
oblige|verb|C1|bắt buộc|to force someone to do something by law or duty|The law can oblige companies to publish their accounts.
